Privacy Policy

"AnythingTimer" (hereinafter referred to as the "App") is a timer application provided by Naoya Hontani (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

The Developer establishes the following policy regarding the handling of user information in the App.

In the event of any discrepancy or inconsistency between the Japanese version and the English version of this Privacy Policy, the Japanese version shall prevail. The English version is provided for reference purposes only.

1. Basic Policy

The App does not provide any functionality that directly collects personal information such as names, addresses, telephone numbers, or email addresses from users.

The App may store timers, app settings, and other data on the user's device for the purpose of providing the App's functionality.

The App also uses services provided by Apple and Google for the provision of certain functionality and the delivery of advertisements. The privacy policies and other terms of the respective service providers apply to the handling of information by those services.

2. Data Stored on the Device

The App stores timers, timer groups, app settings, and other data necessary for the functionality of the App on the user's device.

These data are used for the purpose of providing the functionality of the App.

3. Use of iCloud / CloudKit

The App uses iCloud / CloudKit provided by Apple to provide a function for backing up App data and settings.

Backup data may include timers and timer groups created by the user, as well as other App data and settings.

Data stored in iCloud / CloudKit is handled in accordance with the mechanisms provided by Apple for iCloud / CloudKit.

For information regarding iCloud and the handling of information by Apple, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy and the terms of use for the respective services.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, for the delivery of advertisements.

Information necessary for displaying and delivering advertisements may be handled through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to obtain the user's permission for tracking for advertising purposes.

If Tracking Is Allowed

If the user allows tracking, Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers and other information for purposes such as ad personalization.

If Tracking Is Not Allowed

If the user does not allow tracking, non-personalized advertisements will be displayed.

For information regarding Google's handling of information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and information regarding Google AdMob.

5. Privacy Information Regarding the Advertising SDK

The Google Mobile Ads SDK used by the App includes a privacy manifest provided by Google.

Data handled by the advertising SDK is appropriately managed based on the Google Mobile Ads SDK and related information provided by Google.

6. Analytics

The App does not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or any other analytics services.

7. Subscriptions

The App offers paid subscriptions for certain features.

The subscriptions include monthly and annual plans.

Subscription purchases and payments are processed through the App Store and StoreKit provided by Apple.

The Developer does not directly collect or store users' credit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment information.

The mechanisms and terms of use provided by Apple for the App Store apply to subscription pricing, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and other matters.

8. Siri and App Intents

The App uses Siri and App Intents provided by Apple to provide functionality that allows users to start, pause, resume, and cancel timers and perform other operations.

Information necessary for operations through Siri and App Intents is used for the purpose of executing the functionality of the App.

9. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

Except where required by law, the Developer does not provide personal information directly collected from users to third parties.

However, services provided by Apple, Google, and other service providers that are used to provide the functionality of the App may handle necessary information in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

10. Management of Information

The Developer strives to manage information handled in connection with the App in a reasonable and appropriate manner to prevent unauthorized access, loss, leakage, and other incidents.

11. User Settings

Users can change their permission for app tracking through the iOS settings.

Users can also manage their use of iCloud through their own Apple Account and iCloud settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may modify this Privacy Policy as necessary.

If there are significant changes, the Developer will notify users through the App, this page, or other appropriate means.

13. Contact

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Developer through the contact function provided in the App.
